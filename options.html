Rayna Auto-Dialer — Options

Scripts & message templates

Personal to this browser — each team member sets their own (put YOUR name in the WhatsApp intro). [Client Name] is replaced with the lead's first name. Clearing a box restores the built-in default on Save. The Gmail body is not here by design: it lives in your Gmail draft (set its subject above) so it keeps rich formatting and images — the tool only fills To and the [Client Name] salutation.

Pre-stage images

Optional; stored only in this browser (keep each under ~1 MB). WhatsApp: the image is pasted into the chat after the message — WhatsApp shows its media preview with your text as the caption; you still press Send. Gmail: inserted inline into the compose body (compose mode — a draft carries its own image). The paste is identical to you pressing Cmd+V; nothing is ever sent.
Hotkeys (Voicemail / Live / Pre-stage / Skip / Resume / Quit) are configured at chrome://extensions/shortcuts. Selectors and message templates live in config.js inside the extension folder.

Call listening (beta)

Hears your microphone only — never the lead's audio. Matches distinctive phrases you say (RSVP close, decline wrap-up, voicemail message) and shows a suggestion with a timestamp in the side panel. It never logs, hangs up, or advances on its own. webspeech mode uses the browser's recognizer (audio may be processed by the browser vendor); local_server mode sends 5-second chunks to a Whisper server on this machine only (Voicebox, OmniVoice Studio, or whisper.cpp — the extension refuses any non-localhost URL). Cue phrases live in config.js CALL_CUES.

Self-learning

When a selector fails and you do the action manually during the pause, the click is learned as a fallback. Unrecognized failure toasts are collected below for you to map. Answered-call ring times are recorded to suggest a better ring timeout. Everything is stored only in this browser and never learns Send buttons or judgment steps.
